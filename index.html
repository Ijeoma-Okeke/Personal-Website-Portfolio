About Me

The person behind the work

I'm a writer who fell in love with technology. After years of crafting words that move people, I stepped into the world of machine learning and deep learning, and found that the two disciplines complement each other beautifully.

Today I sit at that rare intersection: someone who can build intelligent systems and also explain them with clarity and warmth. My projects reflect both sides in functionality and thoughtfulness, and are designed for real humans.

I believe that the best technology serves people quietly and faithfully. Whether detecting a phishing scam before it harms someone, preserving centuries of devotional wisdom, or keeping electronics out of landfills, every line of code I write has a purpose.
