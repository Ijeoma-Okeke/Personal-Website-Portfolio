ℹ️ Our Story

We build
for repairers.

Parts Salvage Marketplace was born from a simple observation: the global repair economy is broken — not because people don't want to fix things, but because sourcing the right part is impossibly hard.

⚠️ The Problem

50 billion kilograms of e-waste. Most of it didn't have to die.

53.6M
Metric tons of e-waste Generated globally per year — rising 2 million tons annually with no sign of slowing.
73%
Avoidable discard Appliances thrown away because a single part failed — the rest of the machine perfectly fine.
$62B
Unnecessary spend On new appliances annually that could have been repaired for under $20 with the right part.

"iFixit tells you how to fix something. But when the part doesn't exist in any store, isn't made anymore, or costs more than the machine itself — the repair dies. Parts Salvage Marketplace solves the sourcing problem. Globally. Precisely. Now."

🎯 Vision & Mission

A world where nothing is thrown away because a part couldn't be found.

Our vision is a circular economy where every broken machine becomes a source of parts for another — and where any broken component can be identified, sourced, or fabricated within minutes, from anywhere on earth.

Our mission is to build the world's most comprehensive repair intelligence platform — combining AI vision, 3D manufacturing, global salvage networks, and community knowledge into a single frictionless tool.

We build for the grandmother in Lagos, the engineer in Seoul, the student in São Paulo — anyone with a broken machine and the will to fix it.
